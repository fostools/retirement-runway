      Disclaimer. Retirement Runway is an educational calculator, not financial, investment, tax or legal advice, and it does not recommend any specific security, product or insurance policy. Its results are estimates that depend entirely on the numbers and assumptions you enter; actual returns, inflation, tax law, healthcare costs and benefits will differ, and past or assumed returns do not guarantee future results. Tax figures use simplified rules and 2026 federal brackets and may not reflect your situation. Your inputs stay in your browser and are not sent to any server; this site has no accounts, analytics or cookies. A scenario link contains the numbers you entered, so share it only with people you trust. Before acting on these results, talk to a qualified financial planner, tax professional or attorney.

      How it calculates. Spending starts now and is offset by each spouse's take-home pay until they retire. Pre-65 healthcare starts when both have retired (assumes the working spouse's employer plan covers both until then). Flat effective tax rates; 85% of Social Security treated as taxable; withdrawals each half-year in the order cash, brokerage, Roth contributions (before access), traditional, then Roth. Before access, traditional and Roth earnings carry a 10% penalty. Income above spending is reinvested in the brokerage account.
